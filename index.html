FORGEPLAY VR / VISION PRO

Your Vision Pro.
Your Mac. Connected.

ForgePlay VR connects Apple Vision Pro to VR games running on your Mac. A clear place to get started, find help, and understand your data.

FP / VISION
Free appNo adsApple Silicon Mac required
01

Two devices. One experience.

The Vision Pro app is a companion. It needs an Apple Silicon Mac, the separate ForgePlayVR Mac app, and a compatible Steam VR game. It does not run Windows Steam games on Vision Pro alone.

02

Play at your own pace.

Connect the apps, launch Steam and your game on the Mac, then open the VR game screen on Vision Pro. Keep Mac Virtual Display connected during the current playback setup.

03

Free means the app is free.

The Vision Pro app is free and has no ads. Hardware, Steam accounts and games are separate. Available Mac releases are listed on GitHub; game compatibility varies.
