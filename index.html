Game compatibility

For compatible Windows VR games on Steam that use DirectX 11 and OpenVR. DirectX 12 games are not supported, and some games with anti-cheat cannot run. The SteamVR system dashboard and separate overlay apps are not provided. Compatibility and supported controls vary even among DirectX 11 games.

HAND TRACKING

Point. Pinch. Play.

Raise your hand and aim: the game's pointer follows your hand direction. Bring your thumb and index finger together to select. Turn your head to look around the game world.

Hands, gamepad, or both.

In ForgePlay on the Mac, open VR → Devices & Input to select hand/controller mode and where the controller is connected. Input settings lock after Steam starts or the headset connects. Retina can be changed before Steam starts; Game Mode is unavailable while compatibility is being improved.

01

Hand tracking

Aim with your hand and pinch to select. You can switch pinch input on or off separately.

02

Gamepad

Use an Xbox-style controller or DualSense. Choose whether it connects to the Mac or Vision Pro.

03

Hands + gamepad

Use hand tracking and a gamepad together, according to the controls your game supports.

Controls vary by game. A standard gamepad does not replace every VR motion-controller action.

Built around the way you play.

01

Immersive video and head tracking

Displays a supported VR game running in VR mode on Vision Pro. It does not convert ordinary games into immersive VR games.

02

Game audio and voice chat

Hear the game on Vision Pro. Enable the microphone when you want to send voice to the game on your paired Mac.

03

Remembered pairing

Pair once and use the saved device connection next time. Reset it on both apps whenever needed.

04

Share your Steam prefix

The Mac and VR views in the unified ForgePlay app share one runtime and Steam environment. Steam, accounts, games and external libraries are not copied again. Close running Steam and games before switching modes.

05

Launch from the compact Dynamic Island

Switching to the launcher, Mac or VR view opens the Dynamic Island in its compact state. The compact VR Dynamic Island includes Launch Steam and Force stop Wine. The app icon on the right returns to the launcher. If fully collapsed to Fopl only, right-click Fopl to restore it. Folding, minimizing or closing the window does not end a session. Force stop can lose unsaved progress.

06

Game Mode status and Retina

VR uses DXMT. Game Mode is unavailable while compatibility is being improved; Steam and VR games run without it. High-resolution Retina defaults to off and can be changed after stopping Steam. Retina affects Mac Steam/game windows; VR video quality has its own setting.

Look around the game world.

Start on your Mac. Step in with Vision Pro.

  1. Prepare your Mac

    Install ForgePlay on your Mac and choose VR in the launcher. In Steam setup, connect your existing Steam environment or create a new shared environment if this is your first setup.

  2. Connect your devices

    Keep Mac Virtual Display connected. Open the VR view in ForgePlay on the Mac and ForgePlayVP on Vision Pro. For the first connection, enter the current code shown on the Mac.

  3. Start manually

    Choose Launch Steam in the Mac VR view. In Steam, launch a VR game in its VR mode, then press ‘Open VR game screen’. You can press it while the game is waiting for a VR headset.

Requires ForgePlay on an Apple Silicon Mac and ForgePlayVP on Apple Vision Pro, with macOS 27 or later and visionOS 27 or later. Use a compatible Windows VR game from Steam and keep Mac Virtual Display connected during play. This does not convert ordinary games into VR games.
