HAND TRACKING

Point. Pinch. Play.

Raise your hand and aim: the game's pointer follows your hand direction. Bring your thumb and index finger together to select. Turn your head to look around the game world.

Hands, gamepad, or both.

Choose your input mode in the Mac app's device and input settings before starting a session.

01

Hand tracking

Aim with your hand and pinch to select. You can switch pinch input on or off separately.

02

Gamepad

Use an Xbox-style controller or DualSense. Choose whether it connects to the Mac or Vision Pro.

03

Hands + gamepad

Use hand tracking and a gamepad together, according to the controls your game supports.

Controls vary by game. A standard gamepad does not replace every VR motion-controller action.

Look around the game world.

Start on your Mac. Step in with Vision Pro.

  1. Prepare your Mac and game

    Set up Steam in the VR Mac app and install a compatible VR game. Close the Desktop Mac app and its games before reusing a shared environment.

  2. Connect and choose your controls

    Connect Mac Virtual Display and keep it connected. Pair the two VR apps, then choose hands or gamepad and where the controller connects.

  3. Launch the game, then open VR

    Start Steam and the game's VR mode on the Mac. When ready, choose Open VR game screen in the Vision Pro app.

Requires Apple Vision Pro, an Apple Silicon Mac and the separate VR Mac app. This is not standalone Vision Pro gaming. Keep Mac Virtual Display connected in the current setup. Game and anti-cheat compatibility vary.
