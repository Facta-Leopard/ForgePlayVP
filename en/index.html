Game compatibility

For compatible Windows VR games on Steam that use DirectX 11 and OpenVR. DirectX 12 games are not supported, and some games with anti-cheat cannot run. The SteamVR system dashboard and separate overlay apps are not provided. Compatibility and supported controls vary even among DirectX 11 games.

HAND TRACKING

Point. Pinch. Play.

Raise your hand and aim: the game's pointer follows your hand direction. Bring your thumb and index finger together to select. Turn your head to look around the game world.

Hands, gamepad, or both.

Choose hand tracking, a gamepad, or both. Pick the option that matches the controls your game supports.

01

Hand tracking

Aim with your hand and pinch to select. You can switch pinch input on or off separately.

02

Gamepad

Use an Xbox-style controller or DualSense. Choose whether it connects to the Mac or Vision Pro.

03

Hands + gamepad

Use hand tracking and a gamepad together, according to the controls your game supports.

Controls vary by game. A standard gamepad does not replace every VR motion-controller action.

Built around the way you play.

01

Runs on Mac. Played on Vision Pro.

ForgePlay runs Windows VR games on your Mac. ForgePlayVP receives the game’s video and audio on Vision Pro and sends head, hand and gamepad input back to the Mac.

02

Game audio and voice chat

Hear the game on Vision Pro. Turn on the microphone when you want to send your voice to the game on your connected Mac.

03

Ready to reconnect

Select your Mac and enter its pairing code the first time. Later connections use the saved device information.

Look around the game world.

Start on your Mac. Step in with Vision Pro.

  1. Prepare both apps and Steam

    Install ForgePlay on Mac and ForgePlayVP on Vision Pro. For your first setup, we recommend preparing Steam and a compatible VR game in ForgePlay’s Mac view. You can also create a Steam environment in the VR view.

  2. Choose your VR controls

    Close Steam and games running on the Mac, then choose VR in the launcher. Use Steam setup to connect your existing environment. In Devices & Input, choose hands, gamepad, or both, and where the gamepad is connected.

  3. Connect Mac and Vision Pro

    On the same local network, connect Mac Virtual Display. In the Mac’s VR start view, prepare the Vision Pro connection. In ForgePlayVP, select that Mac and enter the code shown on it. Keep both connections active.

  4. Start the VR game, then open its view

    Choose Launch Steam in the Mac’s VR view. Start a VR game in VR mode from Steam, then tap ‘Open VR game screen’ on Vision Pro. You can open it while the game is waiting for a headset.

Requires ForgePlay on an Apple Silicon Mac and ForgePlayVP on Apple Vision Pro, with macOS 27 or later and visionOS 27 or later. Use a compatible Windows VR game from Steam and keep Mac Virtual Display connected during play. This does not convert ordinary games into VR games.
