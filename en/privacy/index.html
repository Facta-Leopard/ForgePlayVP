Privacy Policy

How ForgePlayVP processes information, stores local data and lets you manage permissions.

Last updated · 2026-10-11

01About this policy

Facta-Leopard develops ForgePlayVP for Apple Vision Pro. This policy covers the Vision Pro companion and this support website. The app is free, has no ads, and does not require a separate ForgePlay account.

02What moves between your devices

The app receives compressed game video and audio from the Mac you pair with. Head position and orientation, hand tracking, pinch/controller input, and technical connection or playback information may be sent to that Mac to run the VR experience. Local networking also uses device names, local addresses and connection identifiers. These are not sent to a developer-operated cloud service.

03Permissions and microphone

Local network access finds and connects to your Mac. Authorized head/world and hand tracking align the view and support input. Microphone audio is transmitted to the paired Mac only when you enable the microphone and grant permission; a game may then send voice to its own services or other players. The app does not collect raw eye-gaze data or passthrough camera video.

04What stays on your devices

Pairing credentials stay in each device’s Keychain with iCloud synchronization disabled. The unified Mac app also uses Keychain access configured not to prompt for authorization. Preferences and limited error records stay on the device. With receiver diagnostics enabled, aggregate timings and loss counters are exchanged with the paired Mac; the last summary and up to 30 recent samples may be saved locally at errors or session end. These records contain no video, audio, gaze or hand positions and are not automatically uploaded to the developer. The app provides no game-video or microphone recording feature.

05No advertising or tracking

We do not operate an advertising, analytics or tracking service inside the app, sell personal data, or use your VR activity to build advertising profiles. Other-app descriptions and icons are bundled with the app. External pages open only when you choose a link.

06Your controls and retention

You can disable the microphone and change permissions in visionOS Settings. Reset pairing clears or replaces the remembered peer and authentication data; a local device identifier may remain. Keychain items can survive app removal, so reset pairing on each device beforehand. Error and performance records have a seven-day validity window; expired records are removed when next read, with no background deletion service while the app is closed. New records may replace old ones. Clear error message removes that error record; Clear saved diagnostics on the Mac removes Mac diagnostics. Other settings and data may persist until reset, replaced or removed with app data. Pairing reset does not delete Steam, games or public posts.

07Apple, Steam, games and this website

Apple, Steam and game providers handle their own services under their policies. This static site adds no analytics, advertising scripts or cookies. GitHub hosts the pages and may process IP addresses and other request information under its privacy statement. Following an App Store, GitHub or other external link subjects that visit to the destination's policy.

08Questions and support information

For support or privacy questions, contact Facta-Leopard through ForgePlay Issues. GitHub issues are public: share only information you choose to make public and review logs before attaching them. Never post passwords, pairing codes, personal recordings or unredacted private paths. Ask for a suitable private channel before sharing sensitive details. Submitted posts are handled by GitHub and may be viewed by the maintainer and others.

09Policy updates

If the app's data practices change, this page and its update date will be revised. Store privacy disclosures and in-app explanations should reflect the version being distributed. This policy does not replace the policies of Apple, GitHub, Steam or individual games.
